states either a reward or a disclosure process. Every entry links to the organisation's
own page. Nothing here is a third-party platform listing.

Generated by `scripts/build_program_list.py` from `data/classification.tsv`.

> A published policy is not blanket permission. Read each page before testing and
> respect its eligible-asset list and exclusions.
